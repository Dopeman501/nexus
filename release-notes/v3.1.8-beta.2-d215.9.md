## Scope

Audio Trigger has an independent collection of up to 32 Solid, Animations and Media backgrounds, sensitivity, entry/silence delay and shuffle. It uses the existing multiplex audio feed, advances multiple selections on distinct attacks, and keeps ordinary background/slideshow settings separate. Disabling the feature, losing the feed or hiding the background releases its resources. The service validates, deep-clones and persists the optional contract through records, presets and profiles, including personalization reset and both AOT JSON contexts.

The component commits contain only the reviewed feature source, tests and translations. Generated packages, dependencies, logs and private application/settings backups remain excluded from Git. Workspace-root knowledge/task records are outside these repositories; the versioned release record owns the source checkpoint summary.
